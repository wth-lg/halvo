# Documented misses

1. Card: a number that fails the Luhn check; fewer than 13 or more than 19 digits; between two digits, anything other than one space or one hyphen: two separators, or any other character, seen or unseen (a no-break space, a non-breaking hyphen, a zero-width character, a direction mark, a combining mark); a digit outside ASCII; a number that an ASCII digit touches, before its first digit or after its last, on a side that C7.3 item 2 does not close.
2. IBAN: lower-case letters; between two characters, anything other than one space: two spaces, or any other character, seen or unseen (a no-break space, a zero-width character, a direction mark, a combining mark); a digit or letter outside ASCII; a number that an ASCII letter or digit touches, before it or after it, on a side that C7.3 item 2 does not close.
3. SSN: a number written without hyphens; between two groups, anything other than one hyphen: any other character, seen or unseen (a non-breaking hyphen, a no-break space, a zero-width character, a direction mark, a combining mark); a digit outside ASCII; a number that an ASCII digit touches, before it or after it, on a side that C7.3 item 2 does not close.

A string that passes a rule and is no identifier is masked all the same; that is a false mask (C7.11). A run of digit groups joined by single spaces or hyphens that touches a masked identifier, or stands one space or one hyphen from it, can be masked with it as a whole, however long the run is, in both directions, as the year of the fifth example of C7.8 is; an IBAN can take upper-case codes after it in the same way; that is a false mask too.
