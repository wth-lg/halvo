# The ten slices: what CI runs outside the merge gate. A slice runs only by hand: the event
# workflow_dispatch of .github/workflows/ci.yml with the input slice, one slice a run.
# sh scripts/ci/run-ci-d.sh <job> --slice <slice> runs the lines of its slice and job: first the
# self-tests, then the checks. A check or a self-test that no line here and no line of
# scripts/ci/merge-gate.txt names runs in no run of CI.
#
# A line is: <slice> <job> <kind> <name>
#   slice  mon, tue, tue2, wed, wed2, thu, fri, sat, sun or pages
#   job    macos or windows
#   kind   check      the check of that name
#          self-test  the self-test of the check of that name (no line names one since r8 cut-2: a run by
#                     hand with the input checks runs a check with its self-test first)
#          tests      12-test in its group form: the tests that scripts/ci/slow-tests.txt gives the
#                     group of that capital letter, and no other test; one tests line a slice and job
# A job of a slice ends in under 1,200 s, the owner's bound for every job of every run. Before you
# add a line, name the seconds of the check in that job: the newest reading of the slice's job stays
# under 1,000 s with them, or another slice takes the line. scripts/ci/README.md, The slices, holds the
# measured seconds and what each slice builds.
# One job stands over 1,000 s by its newest reading and stays under the owner's bound: the windows job
# of wed, whose one row, 50-windows-functional, took 892 s of a job of 1,053 s on an AMD EPYC 7763 in
# the run 45719997737.
