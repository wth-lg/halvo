- Added: On macOS the capture service reads which permissions Halvo holds, by a check that only reads and never shows the system's question, and serves the Permissions page's two requests: Ask raises the system's question once, from one short-lived child of the service, and records that Halvo asked, so the next status shows the permission as asked; putting away a permission that reads Lost takes it out of the record of grants once held.
- Changed: On macOS the screen and input sources begin only after the capture service has read their permission as granted in its current run; until then capture reads starting, and a source whose permission reads not granted shows as failed for that permission. While capture is paused the service still reads the permissions that the switches need, a new grant restarts the service also then, and a permission reads Lost only after the service has read it gone in its current run.
- Fixed: On macOS the capture service that the app starts read no permission: every permission showed as not asked, no source was held back for a missing permission, and a source's own start could make the system ask. A permission check that failed was also started again every tenth of a second instead of after 3 s.
