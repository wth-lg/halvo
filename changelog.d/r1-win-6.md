- Fixed: On Windows the keyboard source's password gate clears a key only on a landing that no earlier key holds: a value-change event that the focused field raised at or after the key's time, read as no password field, with the gate's own read of the focus a hold after it, inside the key's 250 ms. A key typed ahead of a focus move that no latch sees (the program's own move, a page's own move, a field that turns into a password field on a page that holds its thread, an access key) is no longer recorded when it lands in a password field: the five tests of those cases leave the list of known defects and read 0. A landing clears no key that stood before a change of the focus, and in a window that gives one landing a letter (every window whose class does not begin with Chrome_) every key of any class, an automatic repeat too, holds the first free landing made at or after its time, or its thread owes one until that key's 250 ms have passed; only a letter, digit, sign or delete key is ever cleared, so the landing of a paste, an AltGr character or an Enter's new line clears no later letter. The cost: in a classic text box, after Tab, an arrow key, Escape, a shortcut, a dead key or an Enter that makes no new line (or Home, End, Page Up, Page Down, a function key, Backspace at the start of the field or Delete at its end), Halvo notes no letter until you pause for a quarter of a second: letters typed 125 to 250 ms apart are all withheld until that pause, letters closer than about 80 to 125 ms apart lose only the last one before it, and letters further apart than 250 ms lose only the first one after the key; pages in Chrome and Edge pay nothing. A value change of a password field that has lost the focus withholds the keys around it, so where a password manager fills a field, or a page clears its password input, while another field has the focus, the keys of that field within 370 ms are withheld. Seven tests stand as known defects, each beside a twin that records nothing: W6-own-change and W6-own-change-tab (the program changes the focused plain field by itself, works more than 120 ms, and then it, or a Tab typed ahead, moves the focus to a password field where a queued key lands), W6-frozen and W6-frozen-tab (the program is frozen longer than 250 ms while a person types ahead, without or with a Tab in front), W6-pointer-change (a click whose handler pastes into the plain field, then a late move), and W6-earlier-key in Chrome and in Edge (a page folds the landings of a burst into one; the page case shows itself in some runs and not in others, so its two tests stand in the list's long form, which no hosted check runs, while their twins run in every run).
- Changed: On Windows a key behind an open latch (after Tab, Enter, Escape, a Windows key or a button-down) is recorded on its own landing made at or after that latch, however long after the latch, inside the key's own 250 ms, so every letter is noted in a text of several lines behind an Enter, behind a click into one's own text, and in a chat's box that keeps the focus behind Enter.
- Added: The status of a daemon whose keyboard source runs on Windows carries `typing_gate` (`ok`, `field_unknown` or `latch_open`, which it reads from a latch until the first landing made after it), and `halvo-capture ctl status` prints it as one line behind `keys_withheld`; a Mac reports none.
