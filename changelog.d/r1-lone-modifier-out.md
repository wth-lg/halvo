- Changed: Where typing is on, Halvo no longer notes a press of a modifier key, on a Mac or on Windows: Shift, Caps Lock, Control, Option or Alt, Command or the Windows key, and on a Mac Fn, whether you press it alone, hold it or press another key with it. The key that you press with it is noted with its kind as before, so what Halvo keeps no longer shows where you pressed Shift among the letters, digits and signs.
