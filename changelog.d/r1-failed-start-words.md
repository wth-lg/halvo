- Added: On a Mac, when Halvo stops because of an error, it shows one message of the system, titled Halvo, that says "Halvo stopped because of an error. Open Halvo again." with one OK button: when it cannot start, when it can open neither its window nor its menu bar icon, and when it fails at any later moment while it runs, where its window and its menu bar icon may still be on the screen but no longer answer. The message leaves by itself after 60 seconds, and Halvo then ends. A start in the background, as at login, and a start from a terminal or a script show no message. On Windows the same message now also shows when Halvo can open neither its window nor its tray icon, and Halvo ends at the latest 60 seconds after the message appeared, answered or not, except on a computer so short of resources that Halvo cannot start the message on its own: then the message waits until the person presses OK.
