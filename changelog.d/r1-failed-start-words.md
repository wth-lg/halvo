- Added: On a Mac, when Halvo stops because of an error as a person opens it (it cannot start, or it can open neither its window nor its menu bar icon), Halvo shows one message of the system, titled Halvo, that says "Halvo stopped because of an error. Open Halvo again." with one OK button; the message leaves by itself after 60 seconds, and Halvo then ends. A start in the background, as at login, and a start from a terminal or a script show no message. On Windows the same message now also shows when Halvo can open neither its window nor its tray icon, and Halvo ends at the latest 60 seconds after the message appeared, answered or not.
