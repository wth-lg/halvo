- Added: (capture run, daemon-3c) A service run starts its sources only while desired is running, the store's slot is Open and the indicator lease is fresh, read once more after the owner closes its pause, and a start while the drain fails begins no source until the drain heals. A lapse of the lease stops every source through the stop for capture off within INDICATOR_LEASE_MS plus one tick when the owner answers its barrier within that tick (the stop waits for the barrier for up to 5 s before its first stop), stores no record received at or after the lapse barrier, a barrier drawn at the first tick after the deadline, and writes no state; the Board's capture reads NoIndicator. On macOS, `halvo-capture --service-run --lifeline [--data-dir DIR]` (debug builds alone) ends through its one shutdown entry with 0, 1 when the final flush failed, or 5 when a source does not stop, once its standard input reaches its end or its parent is gone; a standard input that is no pipe exits 64. Outside a test build nothing renews the lease until daemon-3e, so a service run still starts no source.
- Changed: (capture run, daemon-3c) A stop for capture off that meets a source that does not stop hands its reply, and every later Board, a capture that reads Pausing in place of Stopped.
