- Added: (capture run, daemon-3c) A service run starts its sources only while desired is running, the store's slot is Open and the indicator lease is fresh; a lapse of the lease stops every source within INDICATOR_LEASE_MS plus one tick through the stop for capture off, stores no record received at or after its barrier and writes no state, and the Board reads `no_indicator`. On macOS, `halvo-capture --service-run --lifeline [--data-dir DIR]` (debug builds alone) ends through its one shutdown entry with 0, or 1 when the final flush failed, once its standard input reaches its end or its parent is gone; a standard input that is no pipe exits 64. Outside a test build nothing renews the lease until daemon-3e, so a service run still starts no source.
- Changed: (capture run, daemon-3c) A stop for capture off that meets a source that does not stop hands its reply, and every later Board, `capture=pausing` in place of `stopped`.
