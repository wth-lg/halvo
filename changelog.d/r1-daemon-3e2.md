- Added: The capture service deletes everything on request: `halvo-capture ctl delete-all (STORE_ID | -)` deletes the store and every registered entry of the data directory while the service runs, also a store that cannot open, and a fresh store stands after it. `ctl set-cap BYTES` sets the storage cap between 1 GiB and 1 TiB and evicts the oldest records down to it at once. On macOS a release build has `--service-run`, `ctl` and `--delete-all --data-dir`, so a shipped app can drive the capture service; the test modes stay in debug builds.
- Fixed: A state file that could not be read gave the default cap of 25 GiB as if it had been read, so the service could evict records on the word of a file that it could not read; now the cap reads unknown and nothing is evicted until a valid cap is set, and only a delete of everything writes fresh defaults over such a file. A delete that another process held back no longer clears the mark that a store was made, and a service that holds a store for maintenance alone reads its stop and its requests between shred calls.
