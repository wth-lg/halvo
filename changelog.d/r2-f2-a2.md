- Changed: The search for repeated tasks knows 86 more words of window titles: more words for a bill, a rent or a lease, an applicant, a mail, a workbook, an order, a calendar entry and a ride, more words that say that a task was done (such as a receipt, a transfer, a countersigned lease, an applicant accepted or denied, an edited workbook, a thank-you for a purchase, reminders cleared or a minicab dispatched), and more words that alone name the page of a chart (such as stats). A page that a person only reads can still be named where its own title holds a word of the task and a word of the task done, such as a mail that confirms an order, read in web mail, or a single receipt opened from a list; a page whose title holds no such pair names no task.
