- Changed: The search for repeated tasks knows 92 more words of window titles: more words for a bill, a rent or a lease, an applicant, a mail, a workbook, an order, a calendar entry, a ride and a chart, and more words that say that a task was done (such as a receipt, a transfer, a countersigned lease, an approved or declined applicant, a reply, an edited workbook, a thank-you for a purchase, reminders cleared, a minicab dispatched, or stats). A list of receipts or transfers that a person only looks at still names no task; a page whose own title holds a word of the task and a word of the task done, such as a mail that confirms an order, read in web mail, can be named although the person only read it.
