- Fixed: (capture run, r1 win-switch-move) On Windows, where Halvo records window titles, a switch to another window now counts as a move of the cursor, as a click, a focus key and a focus event do: a window that you enter from a password field with no click, no key and no focus event is read by its own field a moment after it comes in front, so its title is recorded where that read finds no password field, and what you type in a plain field there is no longer left out until a click, a focus key or a focus event. Where that read finds a password field, Halvo records no key and no title there, as before.
