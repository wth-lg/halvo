- Changed: On a Mac the capture service also leaves out a process that a run's mark under `agents/marks` names by its process number and creation time, and every process under it, for every kind, as long as it runs: a browser whose runner has ended stays out, also after its mark is removed, and a service that the app did not start leaves agents' browsers out as well. The service lists the marks once a second on one thread; no decision lists them, and a start time is read only for a process number that a mark names. Where the folder of the marks cannot be listed or a mark's bytes cannot be read, every decision drops as for an unknown parent until a listing reads them again, and the service writes `halvo-capture: agent_marks=unread error=<kind>` to its standard error when that begins and `halvo-capture: agent_marks=read` when it ends; a file that is no mark is passed. On Windows the marks are not read. `docs/privacy-limits.md` says what Halvo leaves out of an agent's own browser and the limits.
