- Added: On macOS the app starts its capture service as its own child when it opens on a data folder that the capture service has set up before, and on no other folder. It starts the service again when it ends: at once after a restart status that ends a life of 60 s or more, after a wait that doubles from 1 s to 60 s after any other end but a clean one, and no more after a clean end or after a third end in a row that says a source did not stop. The service ends with the app, also when the app is killed. `halvo --background` starts the app with its window hidden, and `halvo --data-dir DIR` opens it on the data folder DIR.
- Changed: On macOS, Quit Halvo in the tray, and Quit Halvo with Cmd+Q in the app's own menu, which takes the place of the default macOS menu, pause capture before the app ends its capture service and exits. Where no capture service can be reached, the app exits at once. Where its own service gives no answer, the app ends that service and shows a sentence, and the next Quit exits. Where a service answers without pausing, the app shows a sentence in the tray and stays open. Before, the tray's Quit exited at once.
