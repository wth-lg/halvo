- Added: On macOS, in builds with debug assertions, the capture service serves its control socket under run/ of its data directory to processes of the same user alone and answers status, inventory, pause, resume and stop, one change at a time; `halvo-capture ctl [--data-dir DIR] VERB` sends one request and prints the answer with the exit codes of ctl, so the app can start, pause, resume and stop the capture. A status that claims the indicator renews the lease without which nothing is recorded, a Mac's status says whether typing is recorded (`typing=off` in this build), and with the feature r2 the service answers the two requests that mark the start and the end of an agent run.
- Fixed: A capture service whose store owner had stopped kept running without recording and without end; it now runs its shutdown and exits 1 with a line that says so. After the indicator lease lapsed and was renewed, a Mac recorded no app in front any more, as after a wake from sleep past the lease; the app in front is recorded again.
