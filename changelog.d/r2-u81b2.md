- Added: (workflows run, U8.1b2) Under the feature `r2`, the adapter of the encrypted capture store answers the cursor's read in crates/halvo-capture/src/store/adapter.rs: the store's id, the highest ordinal given and the first segment at or after a position that holds its key, with its plain lines in one wiped buffer and its expiry instant. A shredded segment is passed with no byte of its file read. A segment whose file is gone or does not authenticate answers no line and is counted in `DataDirStore::damaged_reads`, so the cursor goes on. The read takes no lock and no transaction and writes no file.
- Changed: (workflows run, U8.1b2) The cursor asks the store for at most 64 segments in one pass of the owner (`PASS_SEGMENTS_MAX`), so that a capture write never waits behind more than one bounded read. A read that reaches the bound ends with `next` at the first segment that it did not read, and the client's next read goes on there.
