- Added: (capture run, derived-key) `DataDirStore::derived_store_key`, the one call that hands the daemon the key of the derived store: on a first ask it makes 32 random bytes, wraps them under the root that store/store.key names with the label `derived`, makes derived/ (0700) and publishes derived/master.key (0600) in the store's key-file form, create-only by a hard link from a temporary name of its own, and a later ask, or one whose link lost a race, unwraps that file and answers the same key. It opens no database and takes no lock, never replaces or removes a key file, and answers `DerivedKeyFault`, a variant for each failure (no capture store, a store.key or a master.key that does not read, a master.key that names another root, a root that is gone, damaged, denied or locked, a custody kind that the build does not handle, an I/O fault), with nothing made when the capture store or its root does not answer. A delete of everything destroys that root, so the key cannot be read after it.
