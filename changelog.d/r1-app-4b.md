- Added: On Windows Delete everything works where the capture service does not run, as on a Mac: the app ends its own child, runs halvo-capture.exe beside it with `--delete-all --data-dir` on its data folder, with no console window and no handle beyond its three standard ones, reads its end as on a Mac (0 deleted or nothing stored, 1 deleted and not finished, 75 held by another program) and then, where a capture service ran before the delete, starts it again. At the end of a Delete everything that finished, the app also removes the crash reports of the web view of its own window, which can hold what the window showed; where one stays, the page says "Halvo could not remove a crash report of its own window in {folder}. - Changed: On Windows the system keeps Halvo's own window out of every capture, Halvo's own screen record among them, so a person cannot take a screenshot of Halvo's own window or show it in a screen share; the web view of the window keeps no entry of what a person types into the page and offers none; and from the second unfinished delete in a row the page says "To remove what is left yourself, quit Halvo, open that folder in File Explorer, delete what you do not want to keep, and empty the Recycle Bin." where the Mac names the Finder and the Trash.
- Fixed: On Windows a pause and a resume, or a delete through the capture service, no longer lose the screen unseen: a Windows run of the capture service makes one screen session, so where its status then reads the screen stopped or failed with screen activity on, the app restarts its own capture service once for that Resume or that delete and resumes; where the screen is still not recorded, the page says "Halvo is not recording the screen. Quit Halvo and open it again to record it." A Pause that a person presses while the app waits for its capture service before such a Resume, or before the Resume of a Start after capture was held three times, is no longer undone by that Resume, on Windows and on a Mac.
