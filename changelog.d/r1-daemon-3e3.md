- Fixed: On macOS, Delete everything now finishes when a capture source does not stop: the delete removes the store and every registered entry, makes a fresh store and answers `deleted`, and then the capture service ends with status 5, where it used to answer busy and delete nothing. After an end with 5 from a Pause, a lapse of the lease, a lost permission or such a delete, the next start captures nothing until a Resume, whatever the saved state and the lease say, and its status reads `capture=awaiting_resume`. A Pause pressed while the capture service is ending is now answered and kept, so the next start is paused; a Resume, a claim and a status are answered there too, and nothing starts.
