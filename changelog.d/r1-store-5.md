- Added: (capture run, store-5) The encrypted store can expire old records on time and stay under its cap, through calls that the daemon's owner will make. `expire_due(now, max_segments)` shreds at most `max_segments` live segments whose first capture, floored to whole seconds, plus the window is at or before the caller's now, the oldest first, and returns their ordinals, whether more are due and whether work is owed; it reads no clock and moves no first capture. After a chunk's commit the checkpoint and the removal of exactly that chunk's files are both attempted, the checkpoint once more after the removal when it failed, and a later call finishes what an earlier call owed without a reopen; a commit that fails with SQLITE_FULL removes the chunk's files and runs the transaction once more. `evict_to(bound, max_segments)` shreds the oldest live segments, one at a time, until the bytes on disk are at or under a raw byte bound or no live segment is left, `usage()` gives the bytes on disk and the logical bytes, and `owes_work()` reads whether work is owed. The adapter does not call them yet: expiry and eviction on the real store answer an empty pass until the unit that wires them lands.
- Changed: (capture run, store-5) `EncryptedStore::open` takes the caller's access decision, `OpenFor::Write` or `OpenFor::Maintenance`: a maintenance handle expires, evicts, lists and deletes, and its batch write answers `WriteFault::MaintenanceOnly`; the adapter opens with `OpenFor::Write` for both of its accesses until the next unit. An expiry call removes only its own files and reads no directory, and the keyless sweep runs at open alone. After a pass whose checkpoint failed, a later expiry or eviction call on the same handle finishes the checkpoint; before, only the next open did.
