- Fixed: (capture run, r1 browser-title-readings) On a Mac, a Chrome or Edge that Halvo told as showing English keeps its window titles behind "Resume" and behind every other new start of the title source inside one life of the capture service. Each start of the title source made its decisions about a browser's processes anew and trusted its readings of the language settings only for a process that started later than itself, so a browser lost its titles at every resume until it was quit and opened again. The decisions now live in one handle that the service's factory holds and shares with each foreground source that it makes: one decision for each process, found by the browser, the pid and the start of the process, at most eight. The readings and their epochs are still made anew at each start, so a browser that started while no title source ran keeps no title until it starts again.
- Changed: (capture run, r1 browser-title-readings) docs/privacy-limits.md names each state in which a Chrome or Edge on a Mac has no title recorded until it is quit and opened again, lists the starts of Halvo's capture service, and names as a limit a Chrome or Edge that was started with a language of its own on its command line.
