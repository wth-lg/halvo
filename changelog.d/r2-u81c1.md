- Added: (workflows run, U8.1c1) Under the feature `r2`, the worker slot in crates/halvo-capture/src/daemon/r2/worker_slot.rs: the one place of the capture daemon that starts the analysis worker `halvo-analysis`, under a memory cap of 256 MiB and one at a time, and ends it. A start stops the running owner of the derived store and starts one with the worker's pipe pair; the cursor serves the worker's read channel on a thread of the slot. The slot's caller says with each pass whether a worker may run, from which committed position, and what watermark the cursor last read. An end by the cap, by an exit code other than 0 or by a signal makes the next start at that position wait 1 min, then 10 min, and after the third none starts there; a worker that found nothing to read starts again only when the watermark has passed its position. The slot writes one line for each end. Nothing in the daemon makes the slot yet. For the slot, halvo-cap gains `start` beside `run`: it starts a child that inherits the ends that its `EndsSpec` names and no other descriptor or handle of its starter (on Windows by the list of the handles that it inherits, in the Job), returns at once, and gives a blocking `wait` with the outcome of `run` and an `Ender` that ends the child from another thread.
