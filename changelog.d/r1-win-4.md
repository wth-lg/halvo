- Added: (capture run, win-4) On Windows the capture leaves out the processes under the exclusion root by their lineage (a process snapshot with a creation-time check and a cache) and keeps the titles of browser windows for Edge and Chrome alone: an Edge title only where no bracketed private marker stands before the brand name, a Chrome title only where a UI Automation query tells the window as not Incognito and not in the window's first two seconds, and no title of Firefox or of thirty other browsers known by the file name of their program, while a browser in no list is recorded like any app; each such drop is counted at the source. The installed-software inventory lists the packaged apps of the package manager, and a site reader point for a later run stands under the feature r2.
- Changed: (capture run, win-4) Every Windows source builds its events through the content floor's constructors after the exclusion decision: a keystroke carries its class and no key code, a press is one click with its button, movement, drag and scroll are folded into one record for each class in each 1,000 ms, a title's text goes to the title constructor with its reason, and a screen record holds no pixel and no size and is made only for a frame with a changed region; a pointer sample is decided on the process of the window under the pointer; the keyboard, pointer, title and screen sources answer Off while their switch is off (screen_activity is off by default), and no title and no screen record leaves while a keyboard source's gate reads Password. After a stop is asked, no key, pointer, title or foreground message that waits in a source's queue becomes a record and the open folds and held keys are dropped, their pointer events counted in pointer_dropped_at_stop; a record that a source had already handed on before the stop can still reach the store.
- Fixed: (capture run, win-4) On Windows the title of a private Edge window whose text was over 1,024 bytes was kept, and a title that the source cut was stored as not cut; the ghost window of a hung window carried its caption out; a fold was dropped whole when its last process ended, and a fold that a full sink handed back was stored under another app; a screen frame was kept with no app known to be in front, and every event of a process whose image could not be read, or a title whose process was not resolved, was kept; a process outside the exclusion tree was dropped as one in the tree when a child of the root had taken its parent's id again; and the installed-software watcher now wakes on a key made after it was armed, arms each key again before a wait returns and has a stop event, while the scan fails when no key opens, stops at the first enumeration error, drops an entry that names ParentKeyName and reads no per-user WOW6432Node key.
