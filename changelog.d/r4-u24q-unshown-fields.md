- Added: In halvo-coord (interface run, U2.4q), an effect whose last state is unknown, whatever its cause, gives one entry on the agent's card, in its place in the run, that says that Halvo could not tell whether it went through: run.effect.<class>.unknown for the seven classes with an effect row and run.effect.unknown for the rest, with each kept field of its class that the row keeps once and whose value the reader takes whole, a value that holds no byte and the items of a delete never among them, through the new constructor CardEffect::unknown (ruling RD-126 item 10).
- Changed: In halvo-coord (interface run, U2.4q), the entry of a committed effect that the card's reader refuses shows each kept field of its class that the row keeps once and whose value the reader takes whole, a value that holds no byte never among them, under the new key run.effect.<class>.partial for the six classes that show fields; an entry with no such field keeps run.effect.<class>.unshown or run.effect.unshown and no field, and no byte of a value that does not stand reaches the reply. CardEffect::unshown takes the kept fields (ruling RD-52 (h) 4).
