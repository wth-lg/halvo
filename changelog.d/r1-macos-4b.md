- Changed: (capture run, macos-4b) On a Mac the titles of Chrome, Edge and Safari windows are recorded again, but only for a browser process that runs in English, the localization `en` of its bundle, which the system's own function picks from the browser's AppleLanguages value or else the person's global list; under any other language, at a failed reading, for a process that started before the capture source began or before the last change of either value that a reading saw, and for Firefox and every other browser, no title is read. A browser is also every app that the system lists as a handler of https links; where that list was read, an app under a whole domain of the closed list (org.mozilla., org.torproject., org.chromium.) is a browser only when the list names it, so Thunderbird's titles are recorded again.
- Security: (capture run, macos-4b) A title of Chrome, Edge or Safari in English that ends with that browser's words for a private window, "(Incognito)", "(InPrivate)" or ", Private Browsing", read from the last 64 UTF-16 units of the title, makes no record; the reads of preferences, of the list of handlers and of a browser's process run on the title thread alone, never on the tap thread.
