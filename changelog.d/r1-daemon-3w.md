- Changed: (capture run, daemon-3w) On macOS in a debug build, the owner's expiry and eviction run on the real store through the adapter, where they answered an empty pass: a segment past its window of 14 days stops decrypting within 60 s of that time while the Mac stays awake, and 600 to 660 s of waking time after it when the Mac slept or the wall clock stepped forward by more than 60 s; a start over a backlog expires it in chunks of at most 64 segments, and after each flush, and at a start whose disk total is above the storage cap, the oldest segments are shredded until the total is at or under the cap. The disk and logical totals are the store's own usage(). A stored cap out of range evicts nothing, where the default cap stood in for it.
- Added: (capture run, daemon-3w) A service run that holds a store of the system's own custody that a macOS build with the secure-enclave feature opens for maintenance alone expires its due segments at its start and at each 60 s deadline and evicts it under the cap, one shred call a turn of its loop, and writes no batch; the adapter opens such a store with the store's maintenance access, which refuses every batch.
