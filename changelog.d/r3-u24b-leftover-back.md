- Fixed: after a removal that a kill ended between the move aside and the removal, `halvo_checkpoint::remove` moves the checkpoint left under its removing name back under its own name, by a rename that replaces no entry, when its bytes are no longer what the row expects (as after the person edited the workbook) and that name holds no entry, and answers `left` with `changed`.
- Added: `halvo_checkpoint::Removal::LeftAside`, a `left` answer whose bytes lie under the removing name (a leftover that stays there while the copy's name is empty, the `name` answer of the move aside, a moved copy that the system refused to put back); `Removal::Left` names the copy's own name, or a call that never reached the copy.
- Changed: on macOS `halvo_checkpoint::FileId` reads the volume's kind (`f_fstypename` of `fstatfs` and `statfs`) and counts an inode as stable on `apfs` and `hfs` alone, so `same_file` also compares length and times on `msdos`, `exfat`, `smbfs`, any other kind and after a failed read.
