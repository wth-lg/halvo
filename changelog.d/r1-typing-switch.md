- Added: You can now turn typing off in Halvo and keep the rest of capture, on a Mac and on Windows: the page of what Halvo keeps has a control, Record typing, beside its row of keys. While typing is off Halvo records no key, and the row says "Halvo does not record what you type."; before you turn it on again, the page shows what Halvo will then record. Where Halvo's capture service started with typing off, turning typing on starts the capture service again, so recording stops for the moment in which it starts.
