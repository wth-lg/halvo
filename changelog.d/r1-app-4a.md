- Added: On Windows the app starts its capture service, halvo-capture.exe beside halvo.exe, as its own child with no console window when it opens on a data folder that the capture service has set up before, and on no other folder before the Start step; the child inherits its three standard handles and no other, and its standard input is a pipe whose write end the app alone holds. After a stop of the capture service that the app did not ask for, no child starts until a press on Start or the next launch; Quit Halvo in the tray pauses capture and ends the child before the app exits, and a kill of the app ends the child through its pipe. A Windows run of the capture service that Windows did not take out of its error reporting ends with an exit status of its own, 6, before it reads the key or opens the store, and the app starts no child again by itself after it. The check scripts/r1/ci.d/34-app-windows-start.sh runs the built app on a Windows runner.

Windows refused the setting that keeps Halvo's data out of error reports." after the service ended with status 6, also with Start; the tray says "Halvo is not recording". On Windows the row on typing reads "Halvo does not record what you type.", and the row on passwords, while no typing is observed, says that Halvo records no window title and no screen activity while it can tell that a password field has the focus.
