- Added: On Windows the app starts its capture service, halvo-capture.exe beside halvo.exe, as its own child with no console window when it opens on a data folder that the capture service has set up before, and on no other folder before the Start step; the child inherits its three standard handles and no other, and its standard input is a pipe whose write end the app alone holds. After a stop of the capture service that the app did not ask for, no child starts until a press on Start or the next launch; Quit Halvo in the tray pauses capture and ends the child before the app exits, and a kill of the app ends the child through its pipe. The check scripts/r1/ci.d/34-app-windows-start.sh runs the built app on a Windows runner.
