- Added: On Windows, in the debug builds that run the capture service, the service runs the password-field detector on a thread of its own, with no keyboard source, from the start of its first source of window titles or screen activity to the stop of its last one. Window titles and screen activity read the detector by a reading of their own: the focused element is clear where it answers that it is no password field, and unknown in Firefox, where it gives no answer or cannot be read, and after a switch of the desktop; the reading of keys does not change. Where the detector does not start, or until its first read lands when that read is late, window titles and screen activity are dropped, and the service says so once on its error output.
- Security: On Windows, Halvo keeps a window title or a screen activity record only where it reads that the focused element is no password field. Where it cannot tell, as in Firefox and in a program that gives no answer, it now keeps neither; before, it kept both there. Keys are withheld as before.
