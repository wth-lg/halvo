What Halvo keeps

Halvo keeps a rolling record of your work on this computer, encrypted, and deletes older capture automatically.

Screen, typing, mouse, the app in front and window titles are recorded. Microphone, camera and system audio are off and are never requested in this version. Passwords are never recorded.
